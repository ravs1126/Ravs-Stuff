## What you need

I dont know measurements, i just put stuff until my heart is happy with it :)

- AP Flour
- Sugar
- Baking Powder
- baking soda
- Salt only small amount
- Milk
- Melted Butter 
- Vanilla extract
- egg


## What to do

1. Put the butter to melt in the microwave. 

2. Mix together the dry stuff, flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt and give it a whisk to combine it all together. 

3. after the butter done melt and cooled off a bit, add in the milk, vanilla extract, and the egg, and whisk this to combine it all.

4. Combine the 2 mixtures now, folding it all together. You want the batter to be smooth but with some lumps, like thick and creamy. Once mixed let the chap rest for a little bit.
	- If when time to cook and the batter is TOO thick and wont pour properly just add in a little milk at a time and fold it in till its the consistency you want.
	
5. Preheat the griddle, tawa, pan whatever you gona use. When time to cook, butter the cooking device you use and pour in the batter and give it a little spread around to your desired shape. I use a 1/4 measuring cup to pour each pancake. 

6. Cause the batter was kinda thick, Cook it for around 2-3 min a side, and keep flippin the pancake until its fully cooked through. repeat this till all the pancake done cook.
		- If you want to add toppings like fruits, chocolate chips. when you pour the batter onto the pan, just add in the toppings and use a knife or osmething to push the toppings into the batter and then move the batter around so
		  you cover the toppings as best as you can

7. Now syrup them and eat.
