## What you need

I dont know measurements, i just put stuff until my heart is happy with it :)

#### Egg Mixture
- Egg
- Milk
- Cottage Cheese
- Salt, Pepper
- Spices, use whatever you like

#### Filling

Put whatever you like inside, anything can work

These have:
- Spinach
- Green pepper
- Cheese
- Green onion

## What to do

1. Cook down all the veg, a little bit and set it aside. Not needed can put it into the eggbite raw too.

2. turn on the oven, set it at 350F

3. Blend up the Egg, Cottage Cheese, Sale, pepper, and whatever other spices you want.

4. Grease a uffin tray and then portion your filling into it, or whatever thing you want to cook your bites in. Then pour over the blended egg mixture.

5. Bake for 15-20 min, check it at 15 and see where its at. You know its done when the eggs are set and not jiggly anymore. 

6. Take it out the oven when its done and leave it to sit, then get them out and eat them.

#### My tip for you

- I usually use a knife and run it along the edge of each eggbite if they play bad and dont wanna get out the tray.

- Can freeze them and just microwave them when you wanna eat, I dont know how long they last cause me mama eat them out quick quick
