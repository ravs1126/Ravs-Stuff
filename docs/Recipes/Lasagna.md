## What you need

I dont know measurements, i just put stuff until my heart is happy with it :)

### For Ragu
- Ground Meat (whatever you like)
- Onion
- Carrot
- Celery
- Garlic
- Tomato paste
- Crushed Tomato or tomato sauce or passata
- Milk

### For Fresh pasta

- Just follow the fresh pasta recipe, and instead of cutting it into noods just leave it as whole sheets.

### For Bechamel

- Milk
- Butter
- Nutmeg
- Flour


## What to do

- I usually start off with getting the ragu going, then while that is cooking make the pasta and the bechamel.
- Make sure you season as you go or else the food gonna have no taste

#### Ragu

1. Start cutting up the veg for the ragu. I like to dice up the celery, onion and carrot but if you lazy you can also just grate it. 

2. I like to use a dutch oven. Use some oil and start browning off the meat, I do it in batches so the meat actually gets a chance to brown and doesnt just release all the water and just boil itself.

3. Once all the meat is finished, throw in the onions and cook for a few minutes, then add in the carrots and celery and cook for another couple minute and then graate in or you can also dice the garlic and let that cook off for a minute or so.

4. Now add back in the meat, along with some water about a 1/2 cup or so to help clear off any remaning fond from the bottom of the pot that didnt get released from the veg juices. and cook it until the water is reduced by about half.

5. Add in whatever tomato thign you gonna use, along with some stock or water to get it to a level where the meat is fully covered. Then get it to a simmer, then lower the heat and cook it covered for an hour or two. I typically let it cook until I've finished making the pasta and the bechamel. I like to put a couple bay leaves in here.

6. When the ragu is almost done, add in like 1/4 - 1/2 cup milk and stir it in. The milk gives it a like nice silky and nice taste and mouth feel. Dont have to add if you dont want. 

### For Fresh pasta

- Just follow the fresh pasta recipe, and instead of cutting it into noods just leave it as whole sheets.

- For this recipe I boil the pasta first, so that it will 100% cook in the oven just cause its not a super saucy lasagna so this way theres no worry if the pasta gonna fully cook in the oven.
- Before boiling I like to cut the pasta sheets into the correct length for the dish im gonna build the lasagna inside.
- I boil it for around 90 seconds, then as soon as i take the pasta out the water i drop it in an ice bath to stop the cooking, and then move it to a sheet.

### Bechamel

1. Melt some butter in a pot and then stir in the flour, and let it cook to a light blonde color. 

2. nwo you want co slowly add in the milk, but only when the roux starts to form small bubbles. Start off adding a little bit at a time while whisking it until it thickens and then repeat. You dont want it to be thin and runny, or too thick. You want it to be the consistency of heavy cream, slightly thicker is ok too. 

3. Now season it with salt and pepper, and add in the nutmeg. You dont want to add too mucn nutmeg, it should taste like the nutmeg is a background flavor not like the main flavor. Fresh nutmeg needs less than if you were to use pre ground nutmeg.


### Assmbling the Lasagna

- Now you should have the ragu, lasagna sheets that have been pre cooked, and the bechamel. All you need to do now is grate some cheese, I usually stick to just mozzarells but you can definetly mix and match cheeses.

1. First preheat the oven to 350 F

2. Start off with a thin layer of meat sauce at the bottom of the pan, followed by a layer of pasta.

3. Each layer will be pasta, bechamel, cheese and ragu. Make sure to reserve some cheese for the top of the lasagna.

4. For the last layer the top, just put a layer of pasta with some bechamel on top and I like to mix in a little bit of the ragu on top with the bechamel.

5. Now bake it covered for 35 min. Once the first bake is done, bump the oven up to 400 F uncover the lasagna and add on the reserved cheese to the top. I also like to sprinkle some basil if I have or some dried oregano. Then put it back and let it bake 
for 10-15 minutes or until its a nice folden brown you like. 

6. Before you eat make sure you let the lasagna rest for like 15-20 minutes, or else you mouth will get corruption.
