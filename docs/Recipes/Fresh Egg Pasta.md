## What you need

This one I know measurement :) Ram ram

The measurements here are for a single portion/serving so just scale it depending how big you are :)

100g Flour - I used AP but OO supposed to be the correct one but IMO AP is fine
1 Whole Egg
Pinch salt - If you hand big dont use big pinch or you mouth gonna cry


## Making the dough

1. Make a space in the flour and crack in the eggs, then using a fork beat up the eggs while slowly combining the flour into the eggs

- Pretend youre eating dhal and rice. Put the flour on your work surface like its a pile of rice, then spread it out so you have a nice well for the dhal to go in.
	- The rice is the Flour and the Dhal is the egg
	
2. Once the flour and the eggs are combined enough that the egg wont just run off the work surface to the floor. Start using a bench scraper to cut the egg into the flour (if you dont know what i mean, literally use a bench scraper and start chopping the flour and egg together while bringing it together when it spreads out too much.)

3. When the dough starts coming together you can stop cutting it and use your hands and start kneading the dough. It'll take around 8-10 min of solid kneading. You want the dough to be a nice smoothish ball thing.

4. Once the dough has formed into a nice ball, you can either cover it with a towel and let it rest on the countertop OR you can wrap it in plastic wrap and leave it to rest. Its not gonna swell up like bread dont worry about this. look at the picture to see what my one look like

<img src="/img" alt="wrappeddough" width="350" align="center" />

	#### TIP FROM ME 
 I discovered that the longer the dough rests the nicer it is to work with, so let it rest for around 45 min


## Rolling out pasta

1. Take your belna and start roll the dough as thin as you can get it to be. Once its nice and thin laminate the dough one time. Pretend its divided into thirds, fold one third into the middle then the other third on top. Look at picutre if you get confusion in you

- I did this two times, when you rolling the dough out after laminating dont roll on the folded edge, roll it from the ugly fold side that has the 3 pieces look the picture will explain it 

<img src="/img" alt="laminated" width="350" align="center" />

2. Now just roll the noodle out to your desired thickness, and then cut into the shapes you want to use. I like to make tagliolini, and tagliatelle.

- When youre cutting the noodles into shapes, if you using a machine its fine you get what you get. If youre using a knife cut the noodles slightly thinner than you want it to be when you eat, cause they plump up when you boil the noods


## Cooking the noods

I like to boil the noodles for around 1 minute if I'm boiling it fresh.

If from frozen i boil it for like 1 min 30 sec

boil it to your liking, i prefer the noodle little more toothsome/al dente


## Storage

The noodles can stay around 2-3 days in the fridge in a container, and a long long time in the freezer.

I portion it out into bundles, then lay then on a plate/tray and let it freeze then put it all in a ziplock bag when its frozen.
